Core Insurance Products

Direct strategic advisory for all leading insurance providers. From securing life dependencies to shielding healthcare costs and general corporate assets, we align coverage perfectly to your risks.

Life Insurance

  • Pure Term Insurance: High coverage life security, optional with premium returns.
  • Endowment Maturity: Structured savings plans combined with lifecycle risk protection.
  • Money Back Plans: Periodic cash payouts to support recurring capital objectives.
  • Pension & Retirement: Dedicated income protection systems for post-retirement health.
  • ULIP (Unit Linked Plans): The perfect blend of growth investment and robust life protection.

Health Insurance

  • Pure Mediclaim: Full hospitalisation cashless shields for families & individuals.
  • Personal Accident (PA): Security payouts covering accidental death and disabilities.
  • Cash Allowance Benefit: Continuous daily cash payouts during hospital stays.
  • Critical Illness Shield: Lump-sum payouts upon major life health diagnoses.
  • Overseas Travel Cover: Emergency medical and travel disruption coverage globally.

General Insurance

  • Vehicle Protection: Complete motor third-party and own damage coverage.
  • Asset & Machinery: Shield commercial machinery, goods, and factories from losses.
  • Household Cover: Protection against home damages, fires, burglaries, and natural events.
  • Professional Indemnity: Legal liability coverage tailored for doctors and corporate managers.
  • Public & Employee Liability: Shield operations from public accidents and employee workplace cases.
