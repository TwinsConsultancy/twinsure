Earning Structure

Two Streams.
Every Single Referral.

Transparent, fair, and paid via UPI within 7 days of claim settlement.

Referral Commission — % of Fees Collected

When your referred client's claim is successfully settled, you earn a percentage of our service fee. Bigger claims, bigger commission. Fully recurring — every client referral keeps earning.

Fixed Field Visit Fee — Per Document Collection

Visit a policyholder, collect their claim documents as per our checklist, and hand them over to us. Get paid a fixed fee per visit — regardless of claim outcome. Simple and immediate.

Top Partner Quarterly Bonus

High-performing partners receive quarterly recognition bonuses. No targets required — just stay active and consistent. The top partners in each district get rewarded every 3 months.
